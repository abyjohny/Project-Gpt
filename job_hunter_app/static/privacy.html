Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Job Hunter ("we", "our", or "the application"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our automated job-hunting and resume-matching application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide you with job discovery, resume match analysis, and automated application dispatching:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and Google Account ID to establish your session and workspace.
  • Uploaded Resumes & Documents: Any CV or resume text/PDF files you upload to assess your qualifications against job postings.
  • Search Presets & Application Records: Your target job roles, preferred locations, scan frequencies, and application history.
  • Gmail Access Tokens: When you connect your Gmail account, we store secure OAuth tokens allowing Job Hunter to compose drafts and dispatch application emails at your explicit request.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the core services of Job Hunter:

  • Authenticate you and isolate your private multi-tenant workspace.
  • Scan public job platforms for relevant job opportunities matching your criteria.
  • Analyze the alignment between job descriptions and your resume using AI (Google Gemini).
  • Draft personalized job application cover letters and emails.
  • Dispatch application emails through your authorized Gmail account only when initiated by you or your configured automated runner.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure Job Hunter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Google User Data & Gmail Policy Compliance

In strict adherence to Google's Limited Use requirements:

  • No Advertising: We never use your Google user data or Gmail content to serve advertisements or personalized advertising.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data to data brokers or third parties.
  • No Unauthorized Human Access: No humans read your emails or resume data, unless required by law, necessary for security investigations, or explicitly requested by you for technical troubleshooting.
  • Restricted Scope Usage: Access to Gmail scopes (gmail.send and gmail.readonly) is solely used for sending your job application emails and tracking application delivery.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in isolated per-user directories protected by session encryption. Access tokens are held securely and are never exposed publicly. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and safe token serialization.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full ownership of your data:

  • You can delete your resume, job history, and presets at any time within your workspace.
  • You can disconnect Job Hunter and revoke its Google access at any time through your Google Account Permissions page.
  • To request complete deletion of all associated data from our servers, contact us at the email provided below.

6. Third-Party Services

We utilize the Google Gemini API to analyze job descriptions against resumes and generate tailored cover emails. No resume data sent for analysis is used by third parties to train generalized foundation models outside Google API's enterprise terms.
